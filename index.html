--- layout: home # Index page About Md. Abde Mannaf Md. Abde Mannaf is an academic, researcher, and applied mathematician with an interdisciplinary background spanning Mathematics, Computer Science, Machine Learning, Complex Systems, Nonlinear Dynamics, and Mathematical Modeling. He currently serves as a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Northern University Bangladesh (NUB), Dhaka, where he is involved in teaching, research, curriculum development, and academic activities. He completed his M.S. in Mathematics from Pabna University of Science and Technology (PUST), achieving First Class and securing 2nd position in his cohort. His master's thesis, “Information-Theoretic Analysis of Collective Motion,” reflects his longstanding interest in understanding complex collective systems through mathematical and computational approaches. He previously completed his B.Sc. (Hons.) in Mathematics from PUST. His research interests lie at the intersection of Applied Mathematics, Complex Systems, Collective Behavior, Distributed Information Processing, Multi-Agent Systems, Nonlinear Dynamics, Mathematical Biology, Data-Driven Modeling, and Machine Learning. A major focus of his research is understanding how collective intelligence and emergent behavior arise from interactions among individual agents, including biological and artificial systems. His work combines mathematical modeling, simulation, time-series analysis, information theory, causal inference, and machine-learning techniques. He has contributed to research published in international journals, including venues associated with Elsevier, Wiley, Springer Nature, and other major publishers. His recent research directions increasingly incorporate Machine Learning and Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) with mathematical modeling, particularly for complex dynamical and collective systems. In addition to research, Md. Abde Mannaf has extensive teaching experience in Mathematics, Statistics, Differential Equations, Vector Analysis, Complex Variables, Calculus, Probability, and Computer Science-related subjects. His interdisciplinary teaching experience allows him to connect theoretical mathematical concepts with practical computational and engineering applications. His technical expertise includes Python, MATLAB, R, C, Java, SQL, JavaScript, Mathematica, Maple, COMSOL, Minitab, Linux, Git, and LaTeX. He has particular interest in scientific computing, dynamical-system simulation, agent-based modeling, data analysis, and machine-learning applications. Currently, he is further developing his academic and research profile toward Scientific Machine Learning, Mathematical Modeling, Complex Systems, and Data-Driven Science. He has also pursued international academic opportunities, including the MATHS-DISC Erasmus Mundus Joint Master, where his selected specialization is Scientific Machine Learning (SML). His long-term goal is to establish himself as an internationally active researcher working at the intersection of mathematics, computational science, artificial intelligence, and complex systems. ---
